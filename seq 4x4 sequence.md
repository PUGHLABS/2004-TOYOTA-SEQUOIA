║ 2004 TOYOTA SEQUOIA: 4WD SHIFTING GUIDE ║
==================================================================

🛑 SHIFTING IN TO 4-LOW (LOW RANGE)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. 🛑 STOP -> Bring vehicle to a complete stop on level ground.
2. ⚙️ NEUTRAL -> Move main transmission gear shifter into Neutral (N).
3. 🔘 4HI FIRST -> Press dash 4WD button. Confirm green tire icon is solid.
4. 🕹️ SHIFT LEVER -> Push small 4WD floor lever left and pull down to L.
5. 🔍 VERIFY -> Wait for amber 4LO & VSC OFF dash lights to turn solid.
6. 🚗 DRIVE -> Shift main transmission back into Drive (D) or Reverse (R).

🟢 SHIFTING OUT OF 4-LOW (BACK TO 4-HIGH)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. 🛑 STOP -> Bring vehicle to a complete stop.
2. ⚙️ NEUTRAL -> Move main transmission gear shifter into Neutral (N).
3. 🕹️ SHIFT LEVER -> Push small 4WD floor lever straight up and right to H.
4. 🔍 VERIFY -> Wait for amber 4LO & VSC OFF dash lights to turn off.
5. 🚗 DRIVE -> Shift main transmission back into Drive (D) and resume.

💡 QUICK TIPS FOR STEEP INCLINES & GEAR BINDING
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- ⚠️ IF LIGHT FLASHES: Gears are binding. Keep foot on the brake, drop
  transmission to Reverse (R) for 1 second, then back to Neutral (N).
- 📐 WHEELS STRAIGHT: Always point tires dead straight when shifting.
- 🛠️ MAINTENANCE: Shift into 4LO on gravel monthly to prevent seizing.

❄️ SEVERE WEATHER & OFF-ROAD NOTE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- 🔒 CENTER DIFF LOCK: Press the center differential lock button during
  severe weather or loose trail driving to split power exactly 50/50.
- 🟠 VERIFY DASH LIGHT: Ensure the orange center differential indicator
  light on the dash stays completely solid before entering deep terrain.
